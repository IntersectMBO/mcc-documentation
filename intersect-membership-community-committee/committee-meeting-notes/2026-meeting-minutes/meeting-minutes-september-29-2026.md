## Decisions/Actions

#### Decisions

* #### Workstream Leadership Model: The committee agreed that Q4 will focus on strict "ownership." A workstream lead will act as the primary coordinator and point of contact for an initiative, while other committee members contribute to and support the execution.
* #### Knowledge Base Restructuring: The MCC decided to redesign its Knowledge Base page structure to mirror the layout of the Civic Committee's page.
* #### Translation Approval: Swahili, Indonesian, and German were selected as the primary target languages for the initial localized platform translation push.
* #### AMM Localized Strategy: The committee agreed to center its 2026 Annual Member Meeting (AMM) strategy on funding small, localized community gatherings globally that connect back to the virtual hub.
